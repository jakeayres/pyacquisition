Every experiment has these three tasks, on every task manager, as it has `NewFile`, `WaitFor` and `WaitUntil`. Queue them to pause or slow the measurements at a point in a queue, or save them in a sequence: a pause while a magnet ramps, or a longer period overnight. To do it at once instead, use the top bar's **Measurements** button, or ++ctrl+k++ (**Pause measurements**, **Set measurement period**).
